# Privacy notes for voice practice

Voice recordings can reveal more than words. They may include background sounds, room acoustics, names, emotional state, or health-related clues.

Use these habits when practicing:

- Prefer local processing when possible.
- Avoid uploading samples unless you trust the service and understand its policy.
- Do not share someone else’s recording without permission.
- Keep practice files out of public folders.
- Delete rough takes you do not need.

[Voice Gender Test](https://voicegendertest.com/) is designed around browser-based analysis so users can explore vocal signals without uploading the raw audio sample.
