# Voice practice checklist

## Before recording

- Pick a quiet space.
- Use the same microphone when comparing sessions.
- Keep the microphone distance consistent.
- Decide whether the sample will stay private.
- Stop if your voice feels strained or painful.

## During recording

- Record 10 to 30 seconds.
- Use the same phrase or paragraph for comparisons.
- Speak at a comfortable volume.
- Avoid forcing pitch changes.
- Take a short break between takes.

## After recording

- Note average pitch only as one clue.
- Notice pitch variation across phrases.
- Listen for brightness, warmth, and clarity.
- Compare recordings made under similar conditions.
- Write one small practice focus for next time.
